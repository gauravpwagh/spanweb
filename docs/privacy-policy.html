Link Vault Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

In short: Link Vault collects no personal data. Your links stay on your phone, encrypted, and the app has no internet access. Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

This policy describes how the Link Vault app for Android (package io.github.gauravpwagh.spanweb) and iOS handles your information.

Information we collect

None. Link Vault does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The Android app does not have the internet permission, so it cannot connect to any server.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your links, with the date you added them and any tags and notes you add.
  • A record of wrong PIN attempts, used to slow down guessing.

All of this stays in the app's private storage on your device. Links are encrypted with AES-256 using a key derived from your PIN. Links shared to the app while it is locked are encrypted right away and can only be read after you unlock it. Your PIN itself is never stored.

Backups

Link Vault opts out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so your links are not copied to Google's backup service. To move links to another device, use the app's Export feature.

Export and import

When you export, you choose where the file is saved, and the file is then under your control. An encrypted export can only be opened with the password you set. Plain JSON and plain text exports are not encrypted, and anyone with the file can read them. Importing reads only the file you pick.

Clipboard

When you copy a link, the app places it on your device clipboard and clears it after 60 seconds if it is still there. On Android 13 and later, the copy is marked as sensitive so it is not shown in the clipboard preview. The app never opens links.

Permissions

Link Vault requests no special permissions. It receives text only when you share it to the app, and accesses files only through the system file picker when you choose to import or export.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual links in the app, erase everything with "Forgot PIN?" on the lock screen, or uninstall the app, which removes all its data from your device. Because the developer never receives your data, there is nothing held elsewhere to delete.

Children

Link Vault is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. A future version may offer optional video thumbnails, which would need internet access. That would be opt-in, and this policy would be updated before it is released.
