Link Vault Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

In short: Link Vault collects no personal data. Your links stay on your phone, encrypted. The Play Store edition has no internet access. A preview edition, given only to invited testers, can ask video sites and websites for titles and thumbnails, but only if you turn that on or tap for it. Nothing is ever sent to the developer.

This policy describes how the Link Vault app for Android (package io.github.gauravpwagh.spanweb) and iOS handles your information. It covers two editions of the Android app:

  • The standard edition, published on Google Play. It has no internet access.
  • The preview edition (version names ending in -preview), given only to invited testers through Google Play's internal testing. It adds optional titles and thumbnails, described in Preview edition: titles and thumbnails.

Information we collect

None. Link Vault does not collect, sell or share any personal or usage data, and the developer runs no server. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The standard edition does not have the internet permission, so it cannot connect to anything. The preview edition connects only as described below, directly from your phone to the site concerned.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your links, with the date you added them and any titles, tags and notes. When another app shares a link together with a page title, that title is kept.
  • Domain changes you set up, so links from a website's old address are saved under its new one.
  • In the preview edition, thumbnails you chose to load, and your title and thumbnail settings.
  • A record of wrong PIN attempts, used to slow down guessing.

All of this stays in the app's private storage on your device. Links, titles and thumbnails are encrypted with AES-256 using a key protected by your PIN. Thumbnail file names are derived from a secret key, so they don't reveal which videos you saved. Links shared to the app while it is locked are encrypted right away and can only be read after you unlock it. Your PIN itself is never stored.

Preview edition: titles and thumbnails

These features exist only in the preview edition. They are off by default, and nothing is fetched until you turn them on or tap for them. Nothing is fetched while the app is locked.

  • Video titles and thumbnails (YouTube, Vimeo, Dailymotion). You turn this on, and choose each site, under Video titles & thumbnails. The app then asks that site's official information service about the video (for example www.youtube.com/oembed, vimeo.com/api/oembed.json or www.dailymotion.com/services/oembed), and downloads the thumbnail image from that site's own image servers (i.ytimg.com, i.vimeocdn.com, *.dmcdn.net). This happens when you tap "Load title and thumbnail", or, if you choose it, automatically for newly saved links on those sites. After an import, the app asks before loading anything.
  • Fetch title and image for any other link. When you tap "Fetch title and image from" a website in a link's details, the app requests that one page from that website and reads only its title and the address of its preview image (the picture the page offers for link previews) from the start of the page. It follows redirects only within the same website. If the page names a preview image, the app downloads that one image, which may be kept on a different server chosen by the website (for example an image hosting service).

What those sites receive: the address of the video, page or image the app asked for, your IP address, and a generic browser identifier. The app sends no cookies, no account details, no referrer, and none of your other links, tags or notes. YouTube is operated by Google, and each site handles what it receives under its own privacy policy. Requests are made directly from your phone; the developer receives nothing.

Safeguards: secure connections (HTTPS) only; video requests go only to the services listed above; connections to private or local network addresses are refused; replies are limited in size and time; and every image is re-encoded by the app before it is shown or stored.

Turning it off: switch off Video titles & thumbnails, or a single site, at any time, and use "Delete all thumbnails" to remove stored images, including images fetched from pages. Titles can be edited or cleared in each link's details. The standard edition never makes these requests.

Backups

Link Vault opts out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so your links are not copied to Google's backup service. To move links to another device, use the app's Export feature.

Export and import

When you export, you choose where the file is saved, and the file is then under your control. An encrypted export can only be opened with the password you set. Plain JSON and plain text exports are not encrypted, and anyone with the file can read them. Importing reads only the file you pick.

Clipboard

When you copy a link, the app places it on your device clipboard and clears it after 60 seconds if it is still there. On Android 13 and later, the copy is marked as sensitive so it is not shown in the clipboard preview. The app never opens links.

Permissions

The standard edition requests no special permissions. The preview edition also has the internet permission, used only as described above. Link Vault receives text only when you share it to the app, and accesses files only through the system file picker when you choose to import or export.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual links in the app, erase everything with "Forgot PIN?" on the lock screen, or uninstall the app, which removes all its data from your device. Because the developer never receives your data, there is nothing held elsewhere to delete.

Children

Link Vault is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If titles or thumbnails are ever added to the standard edition, this policy will be updated before that release.
