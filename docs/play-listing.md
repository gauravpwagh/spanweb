## App name (max 30)

Link Vault: Private Links

## Short description (max 80)

Save shared links privately. PIN-locked, encrypted, offline, grouped by website.

## Full description (max 4000)

Link Vault is a private place to keep the links you collect. Share a link from any app, and Link Vault files it under its website, checks whether you already have it, and keeps it encrypted behind your PIN.

SAVE FROM ANY APP, NO PIN NEEDED
Tap Share in YouTube, your browser, Reddit or any other app and pick Link Vault. The link is saved instantly, without unlocking. It is encrypted the moment it arrives and can only be read after you enter your PIN.

ORGANISED FOR YOU
• Links are grouped by website automatically, so youtu.be and m.youtube.com both land under youtube.com
• Add tags, as many as you like per link, from your own tag list or typed freely
• Filter by one or more tags, search links, tags and notes
• Sort by date added, newest or oldest first
• Add a short note to any link

NO DUPLICATES
• The same video or post is recognised even in different link forms: short links, mobile links, share links and tracking parameters are all matched
• Tracking parameters such as utm_source and si are removed when you save
• Links that end the same way on different websites are flagged as possible duplicates, so you can review them

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Always opens on your PIN screen, and locks as soon as you leave the app
• Everything is encrypted on your phone with AES-256, using a key made from your PIN
• Wrong PIN guesses are slowed down with increasing waits
• No internet access at all: the app cannot send your links anywhere
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking
• Screenshots and the recent-apps preview are blocked
• Not included in cloud backups

COPY, NEVER OPEN
Link Vault never opens links itself. You copy a link and paste it where you choose. The copy is hidden from the clipboard preview, and the clipboard is cleared after 60 seconds.

MOVE YOUR LINKS IN BULK
• Export to a password-protected file, plain JSON, or a simple list of links
• Import any of these, or any text file that contains links; duplicates are merged and your tags are kept

PLEASE NOTE
There is no PIN recovery. This keeps your links safe, but if you forget your PIN the only way back in is to erase everything. Export a backup from time to time.
